Previously, I received my Ph.D. from the School of Computer Science and Engineering at Central South University, where I was advised by Prof. <a href="https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=xcQESkIAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN">Zhigang Hu</a> and Academician Jue Zhong. I also conducted visiting research at Western University under the guidance of Prof. <a href='https://sites.google.com/site/borriewang/'>Boyu Wang</a> and Prof. <a href='https://www.csd.uwo.ca/~xling/'>Charles Ling</a>. My research interests include Optimization for Personalized Federated Learning, as well as Deep Learning and Computer Vision in Remote Sensing.

Before that, I received my M.S. degree from Central South University in 2021 and my B.S. degree from Taiyuan University of Technology in 2018.

`I’m looking for motivated collaborators to turn ideas into papers.` Whether you are an undergraduate, master’s, or Ph.D. student, you are welcome to email me. Let’s explore federated learning, remote sensing vision, and interdisciplinary ML problems together.
